Install it on the board

The starter app's own code, its screens and its settings page, running here. Use the mouse, or your finger on a phone, as you would on the display.

Starting…

  • Drag sideways: the hello and system pages
  • Press and hold: Settings (its Wi-Fi row: setup, whose codes work only on the real display)

Its settings page

The page the display serves to your phone, here talking to the display beside it: change the language and watch the screen follow. Your choices stay in this browser. Wi-Fi and updates need the real display.
